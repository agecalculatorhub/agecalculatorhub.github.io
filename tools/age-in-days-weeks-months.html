Age in Days, Weeks & Months

Enter a date of birth and see your age expressed as total days, weeks, months and hours lived.

How it works: days are counted exactly from the calendar, including every leap day. Weeks are days divided by seven. Months are completed calendar months, which is the same method doctors use for babies.
