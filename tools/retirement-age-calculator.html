Retirement Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth and the age you want to retire at, and find your retirement date — plus how many years, months and days you have left to work.

How it works: your retirement date is your date of birth plus the retirement age you enter. This is a planning aid, not financial advice — your state pension age, employer scheme rules and savings all affect when you can actually afford to retire.
