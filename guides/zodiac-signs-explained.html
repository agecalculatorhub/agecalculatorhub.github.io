The 12 Zodiac Signs Explained: Dates, Traits and Elements

AgeCalculator guides

The zodiac is a circle of twelve signs that has been used for more than two thousand years to divide the year and, in astrology, to describe personality. Your sign is determined by your date of birth: if you were born between 21 March and 19 April, you are an Aries, and if you were born between 23 August and 22 September, you are a Virgo. This guide explains all twelve signs, their dates, their elements and the traits traditionally associated with each.

How the zodiac works

The zodiac is based on the Sun's apparent path through the sky over the course of a year. As the Earth orbits the Sun, the Sun appears to move through twelve constellations, spending roughly a month in each. Your Sun sign is the constellation the Sun was in on the day you were born. The twelve signs are Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces, in that order, and they repeat every year.

Each sign is also assigned one of four elements — fire, earth, air or water — and one of three qualities — cardinal, fixed or mutable. Fire signs are Aries, Leo and Sagittarius; earth signs are Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn; air signs are Gemini, Libra and Aquarius; water signs are Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces. The elements are said to describe temperament: fire is passionate and energetic, earth is practical and grounded, air is intellectual and social, and water is emotional and intuitive.

The fire and earth signs

The fire signs are Aries (21 March to 19 April), Leo (23 July to 22 August) and Sagittarius (22 November to 21 December). In traditional astrology, fire signs are described as bold, enthusiastic and energetic, with a love of action and a tendency to act first and think later. Aries is associated with leadership and courage, Leo with confidence and creativity, and Sagittarius with adventure and optimism. Whether or not you believe in astrology, these descriptions have become a shared cultural shorthand for personality.

The earth signs are Taurus (20 April to 20 May), Virgo (23 August to 22 September) and Capricorn (22 December to 19 January). Earth signs are traditionally described as practical, reliable and patient, with a focus on the material world and a preference for steady progress over sudden change. Taurus is associated with loyalty and comfort, Virgo with precision and service, and Capricorn with ambition and discipline. The earth signs are the zodiac's builders and organisers, and their season falls in the quieter parts of the year.

The air and water signs

The air signs are Gemini (21 May to 20 June), Libra (23 September to 22 October) and Aquarius (20 January to 18 February). Air signs are traditionally described as curious, communicative and sociable, with a love of ideas and conversation. Gemini is associated with versatility and wit, Libra with balance and fairness, and Aquarius with originality and independence. In the traditional system, air signs are the thinkers and communicators of the zodiac.

The water signs are Cancer (21 June to 22 July), Scorpio (23 October to 21 November) and Pisces (19 February to 20 March). Water signs are traditionally described as emotional, intuitive and empathetic, with a strong inner life and a deep connection to others. Cancer is associated with home and family, Scorpio with intensity and transformation, and Pisces with imagination and compassion. The water signs complete the cycle, and Pisces ends the zodiacal year just before Aries begins it again.

Signs, science and culture

It is important to be clear: there is no scientific evidence that the position of the Sun at your birth affects your personality, and controlled studies have repeatedly failed to find any such effect. Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science, and the descriptions of the signs are best read as a language for talking about personality rather than a prediction of it. The Barnum effect — our tendency to accept vague, flattering descriptions as personally true — explains much of astrology's appeal.

Even so, the zodiac remains a powerful part of popular culture. Newspaper horoscopes, birthday cards, jewellery and tattoos all use the signs, and knowing your sign is a common piece of small talk. The dates of the signs have also shifted slightly over two thousand years because of the precession of the equinoxes — the constellations have drifted by about a month — but astrologers use the traditional tropical zodiac, which is tied to the seasons rather than the stars. Whatever you believe, the zodiac is a fascinating piece of human history.

Frequently asked questions

What is my zodiac sign?
It depends on your birth date — for example, 21 March to 19 April is Aries.

What are the four zodiac elements?
Fire, earth, air and water — three signs belong to each element.

Is astrology scientifically proven?
No — controlled studies have found no evidence that birth dates predict personality.
